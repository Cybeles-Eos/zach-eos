I started on the frontend. Full-stack came next so I could connect a UI to real data.

That meant learning routes, databases, and how to keep forms, auth, and errors understandable for users.

**What I took from it**

- The frontend is only half of the product
- Good APIs make the UI simpler
- Errors need a clear message, not a blank screen
