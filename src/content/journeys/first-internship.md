This was the first time I worked with designers, other developers, and real deadlines.

I learned how to turn Figma files into pages, how to ask better questions, and how small details (spacing, states, loading) change how a site feels.

**What I took from it**

- Read the design before writing CSS
- Keep components simple so they can be reused
- Ship a working version, then refine
