Freelance work taught me to listen first. Clients usually know the goal, not the layout.

I started with simple landing pages, then moved into multi-page sites with forms, project lists, and clearer navigation.

**What I took from it**

- A clear brief saves hours of rework
- Mobile layout is not an afterthought
- Simple copy and structure beat extra animation
