  - id: what-i-build
    label: What i build
    title: From Figma designs to working dashboard and business features.
    paragraphs:
      - I worked on the project from Figma design to static frontend development. I built parts of the main website and dashboard based on our approved layouts. Inside the system, I helped build the POS, Booking Board, Inventory, Dashboard, and Order Slip pages with the team.
      - For the Booking Board, I helped plan the database structure before we started building the feature. We first studied how rooms, room details, room rates, room status, booking records, and related data should connect. This gave us a clear table structure before adding the Laravel logic.
      - I also worked on the booking status flow and cron job. The cron job checks booking schedules based on their dates and times. It helps keep room statuses updated around check-in and check-out without requiring every update to be done by hand.
